# Matching algorithms

Profile matching uses required coverage (55%), preferred coverage (20%), proficiency (15%), and work preferences (10%). Unavailable criteria are removed and weights redistributed. Names are normalized through the shared taxonomy and aliases. Categories are guidance only: Strong 80–100, Good 65–79, Developing 45–64, and Limited 0–44.

ATS readiness is a separate document-quality estimate: parseability 30%, structure 25%, contact information 15%, skills/evidence 20%, and clarity 10%.

Resume-to-job alignment is also separate. It evaluates required skills 40%, preferred skills 15%, experience/project evidence 20%, responsibility relevance 15%, and education/certification 10% only when the job specifies it. Non-applicable weights are redistributed. The system distinguishes skills listed, skills supported in evidence sections, skills not recognized, and terminology that may require human clarification.

All three engines are deterministic, bounded to 0–100, exclude demographics, and never reject candidates or predict employment.
