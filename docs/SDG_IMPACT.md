# SDG impact framework

- **SDG 4 / Target 4.4:** skill-gap identification and curated learning records support employability skill development.
- **SDG 8 / Target 8.5:** structured skill requirements and opportunity matching may improve the quality of candidate-employer connections.
- **SDG 10 / Target 10.2:** protected traits are excluded, alternative learning pathways are recognized, and score reasoning is visible.

Prototype indicators count profiles, generated match analyses, learning milestones, and applications stored in the platform. They measure product activity only and must not be presented as proof of employment, wage, education, or inequality outcomes.
