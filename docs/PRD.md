# Product requirements

## Smart Job Discovery

Candidates discover attributed internal and authorized external Malaysian opportunities, receive limited-confidence recommendations only when sufficient evidence exists, compare a real resume, save listings, open the original source, and manually track external status. Provider failure and insufficient-data states remain explicit; discovery is never represented as application submission.

SkillBridge AI connects candidate capabilities to explicit employer competency needs. Candidates manage skills, privately upload resumes, review extracted information, understand separate ATS readiness and job-alignment scores, inspect gaps, follow a roadmap, and apply. Employers publish structured roles and review activity.

Acceptance requires persistent role isolation, actual PDF/DOCX extraction, candidate-owned resume records, deterministic non-demographic scoring, visible limitations, responsive interfaces, and executed tests. Semantic AI, OCR, credential verification, live labor feeds, messaging, and automated hiring decisions are outside the MVP.
